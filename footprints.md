# Footprints

Search patterns for finding prospect pages. Replace `[niche]`, `[category]`, `[brand]` and `[competitor]` with real terms (try two or three variants of each).

These are **starting points to test**, not proven patterns. Search operators (`intitle:`, `inurl:`, `site:`, quotes, `-`) are standard Google syntax; some search tools ignore them, so check that the results actually match and fall back to plain phrases when they do not.

Mark a footprint `(works)` or `(dud)` after a run, with the date.

## Resource pages (broken link building, resource pages)

- `[niche] intitle:resources`
- `[niche] intitle:"useful links"`
- `[niche] intitle:"helpful links"`
- `[niche] inurl:resources`
- `[niche] inurl:links`
- `[niche] "useful resources"`
- `[niche] "recommended sites"`
- `[niche] "further reading"`
- `[niche] intitle:"best" "websites"`

## Old guides likely to have rotted links (broken link building)

- `[niche] "ultimate guide"` (prefer results with old publish dates)
- `[niche] "complete guide"` (same)
- `[niche] "list of"`
- `[niche] site:.edu resources` (university and library guides)
- `[niche] site:.gov resources`

## Guest posts

- `[niche] "write for us"`
- `[niche] "guest post guidelines"`
- `[niche] "submit a guest post"`
- `[niche] "become a contributor"`
- `[niche] "contributor guidelines"`

## Unlinked mentions

- `"[brand]" -site:[brand domain]`
- `"[product name]" -site:[brand domain]`
- `"[founder name]" "[brand]" -site:[brand domain]`
- misspellings and spacing variants of the brand, each in quotes

## Listicles ("best X" inclusion)

- `best [category] tools`
- `best [category] software`
- `top [category] tools [current year]`
- `[competitor] alternatives`
- `[competitor] vs`
- `[competitor] competitors`
- `intitle:"best" [category]`

## Moving Man

- `[niche] "rebrands as"`
- `[niche] "has been acquired"`
- `[niche] "shutting down"`
- `[niche] "sunsetting"`
- `[niche] "discontinued"`
- `[competitor or tool] "is now"`
