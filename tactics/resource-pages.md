# Resource pages

Get the user's page added to curated resource and links pages in the niche.

Tools, verdicts and house rules are in [SKILL.md](../SKILL.md). Thresholds are starting values to tune.

## Step 1. Find resource pages

**Do:** resource-page footprints in [footprints.md](../footprints.md) plus niche keywords.

## Step 2. Qualify

**Do:** `blb.py scan PAGE [PAGE ...]` for contact routes, sponsored markers and the modified date. Read the page.

**Pass when the page:**
- actively lists external resources for readers (not a page of ads);
- shows signs of upkeep: a modified date in the last 3 years, or its links mostly work;
- has a section where one of the user's pages genuinely belongs;
- has no "submit your link for a fee" or sponsored markers.

## Step 3. Check for dead links first

**Do:** `blb.py outbound PAGE`. If it has dead links, switch to [broken-link-building.md](broken-link-building.md) step 3. A broken link pitch gets more replies than a plain inclusion request.

## Step 4. Contact

As [broken-link-building.md](broken-link-building.md) step 5.

## Step 5. Draft

Under 100 words. Name the exact section.

```
Subject: Suggestion for your [topic] resources page

Hi [first name],

I came across your [page title] page while looking for [topic] resources. The [section name] section is a great list.

We have a [format] on [topic] that might fit there: [URL]. It covers [one concrete thing their readers get].

Thanks for putting the list together.

[Sender name]
```

One follow-up after about 7 days, then stop.
